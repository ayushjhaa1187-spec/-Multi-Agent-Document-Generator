## 2024-06-12 - Information Leakage via In-Memory Analytics Cache
**Vulnerability:** The in-memory analytics caching mechanism (`AnalyticsTracker` in `lib/analytics.ts`) captured full `Error` objects including sensitive stack traces during `trackError` events. These un-sanitized events were subsequently exposed publicly without authentication via the `app/api/analytics/route.ts` endpoint, resulting in potential sensitive information leakage about the system internals.
**Learning:** In-memory caching, when exposed through unauthenticated public endpoints, acts as a public boundary. Any data, especially errors and metadata, must be strictly sanitized before being stored in such caches to prevent accidental exposure of system internals, while internal observability can be maintained through server-side logging.
**Prevention:** Always sanitize objects, removing properties like `stack` traces or sensitive context data, before pushing them into arrays or structures intended for exposure via metrics or analytic APIs. Maintain full diagnostic visibility by logging complete objects (`console.error`) server-side exclusively.
